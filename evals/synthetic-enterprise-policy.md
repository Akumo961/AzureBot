## Policy exceptions

A policy exception requires a documented business justification, an identified risk owner, an expiration date, and approval from the designated policy owner. Temporary exceptions must be reviewed before their expiration date.

## Evidence standard

The assistant must answer from retrieved evidence. When evidence is missing or conflicting, it must state the limitation rather than inventing a policy requirement.
